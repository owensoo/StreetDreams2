Street Dreams and ECFHNA

Meeting handout for the East Cleveland Forest Hill Neighborhood Association

Street Dreams in one sentence: neighbors on 11 Forest Hill streets are exploring a plan to repave their streets and fix curbs, sidewalks and streetlights through a self imposed special assessment, ideally lowered by a state grant the City would apply for in September 2027.

Two ways ECFHNA could be involved

Option 1: Adopt Street Dreams as an ECFHNA project. ECFHNA leads it, sets direction and speaks for the project. Street Dreams volunteers and materials support its work.

Option 2: Serve as the nonprofit partner. Street Dreams volunteers lead day to day; ECFHNA lends its name and nonprofit status for letters, legal help and small grants.

What either role could involve

Could involve

  • Letters to the City, funders and partners in ECFHNA's name.
  • Being the client for free legal help from the CWRU and CSU law clinics.
  • Receiving small grants for outreach or a senior relief fund, if its 501(c)(3) status is active.
  • ECFHNA members helping with outreach on the 11 streets, alongside Street Dreams volunteers.

Would not involve

  • Collecting or guaranteeing assessments. The City levies them and the County collects them.
  • Paying for the work. Neighbors' assessments and any state grant do.
  • Deciding for any street. Each street's owners decide by petition.

These points would be confirmed with a law clinic before ECFHNA commits to anything.

Key dates

  • January 2027: meetings with the City and potential partners
  • March 31, 2027: each street decides go or no-go
  • April to June 2027: petitions on the streets that decide to go
  • About September 9, 2027: state grant application due
  • About 2029: construction, with payments starting only after the work is done

This is a concept neighbors are still exploring, and details will be confirmed with the City and County.
